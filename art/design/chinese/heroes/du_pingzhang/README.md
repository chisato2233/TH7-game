# Du Pingzhang

Du Pingzhang is an enforcer of imperial law and fiscal order. Where others see resources, he sees obligations.
Cold and methodical, he ensures every territory sustains the stability of the empire.

## Philosophy
- Prosperity without order leads to collapse.
- Every domain must sustain the system it serves.
- Leniency breeds long-term instability.

## Symbolism
- Seals and Tallies: authority and accountability
- Measured Pressure: control through inevitability
