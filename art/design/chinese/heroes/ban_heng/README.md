# Ban Heng

Ban Heng is a military strategist and legal scholar who embodies the disciplined mind of the Han–Tang Civilization.
She believes wars are decided by systems that deny the enemy meaningful choices.

Educated in classical military doctrine and imperial law, Ban Heng treats the battlefield as an extension of governance.
Every maneuver is calculated, and every formation serves a greater design.

## Philosophy
- Victory is achieved before battle begins.
- Disorder is the result of weak leadership.
- Law and strategy are inseparable tools of control.

## Symbolism
- Bamboo Scroll: recorded doctrine and governance
- Bow Motif: precision and restraint
- Upright Posture: authority through composure
