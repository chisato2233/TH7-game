# Guo Anxi

Guo Anxi is an expeditionary commander charged with extending Han–Tang influence beyond its borders.
He does not conquer through annihilation. He imposes systems, restructures territories, and forces rivals into compliance.

## Philosophy
- Expansion without assimilation is meaningless.
- Foreign lands must be reshaped, not destroyed.
- Order is universal, regardless of origin.

## Symbolism
- Marching Banners: authority in motion
- Disciplined Cavalry: speed under control
